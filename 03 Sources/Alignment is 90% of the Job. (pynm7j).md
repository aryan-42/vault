# Alignment is 90% of the Job.

## Why I saved this

_No note._

## Summary

Sinbad argues that alignment—internal clarity, instinct, and state—matters far more than hours worked or 'widgets' produced. He shares how years of marathon/Ironman training were a fear-driven side quest misaligned with his filmmaking mission, and how returning to meditation, sleep, semen retention, and visualization unlocked real progress on his miniseries Riverrats. The core claim: trust your innate 'success instinct' like a squirrel trusts its winter prep; operate from alignment first, then act.

- **Alignment precedes effective action** ^cfkkgv
  Most people overvalue execution (hours, widgets, side quests) and undervalue the internal state—clarity, instinct, visualization—from which the right actions emerge. The 'sharpening the saw' phase is 90% of the result.
